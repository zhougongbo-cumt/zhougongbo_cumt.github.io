Gongbo Zhou

Gongbo Zhou, Ph.D., Professor, and Doctoral Supervisor, is the Director of the Jiangsu Provincial Key Laboratory of High-End Mining Equipment (National Key Laboratory Cultivation Base) and the Leader of the High-Level Innovation Talent Team under the Jiangsu Province “Six Talent Peaks” Program. He currently serves as Executive Council Member of the Mining Machinery Branch of the China Construction Machinery Society, Committee Member of the Intelligent Operation and Maintenance Committee of the Chinese Mechanical Engineering Society, Committee Member of the Rotor Dynamics Professional Committee of the Chinese Society for Vibration Engineering, and Executive Council Member of the Jiangsu Instrument and Control Society. He has been selected for the National Major Talent Program for Young Scholars, the Young Talent Support Program of the China Association for Science and Technology, and the Jiangsu Province “333 Project” for Young and Middle-Aged Leading Talents. He has received several honors and awards, including the National Outstanding Young Engineer Award, the Jiangsu Young and Middle-Aged Experts with Outstanding Contributions Award, the Jiangsu Youth Science and Technology Award (Top Ten Young Science and Technology Stars), and the National Coal Industry Youth Science and Technology Award.

His research mainly focuses on electromechanical equipment design and intelligent operation and maintenance, sensor networks, and robots for special environments. He has led several research projects, including the National Key Research and Development Program of China, the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and the Jiangsu Provincial Outstanding Youth Science Foundation. He has published more than 40 papers as first author or corresponding author in journals such as Information Fusion and IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics. As the first inventor, he has been granted more than 40 domestic and international invention patents. He has received several awards, including the Second Prize of the National Technology Invention Award, the Second Prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, the First Prize of the Anhui Provincial Science and Technology Award, the First Prize of the Jiangsu Provincial Science and Technology Award, and the Second Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award of the Ministry of Education.
